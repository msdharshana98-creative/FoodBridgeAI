🍱 FoodBridgeAI

AI-Powered Food Donation and Redistribution Platform

📌 About the Project

FoodBridgeAI is a platform designed to reduce food waste by connecting food donors with people or organizations in need. It helps users share available food donations and makes food redistribution easier and more efficient.

🎯 Problem Statement

Large amounts of food are wasted every day, while many people struggle to access sufficient food. FoodBridgeAI aims to bridge this gap by connecting food donors with those who need food.

💡 Our Solution

FoodBridgeAI provides a simple platform where:

- Donors can share available food details.
- Users can view available food donations.
- Food information can be accessed easily.
- Food redistribution can be encouraged to reduce food waste.

✨ Features

- Food donation details.
- Display available food donations.
- Find Food option.
- Simple and user-friendly interface.
- Helps reduce food waste.
- Promotes food sharing.

🛠️ Technologies Used

- HTML
- CSS
- JavaScript
- LocalStorage

⚙️ How It Works

1. Open the FoodBridgeAI website.
2. Enter food donation details.
3. Save the donation information.
4. View available food donations.
5. Find food donations through the platform.

🌍 Impact

- Reduces food wastage.
- Supports people in need.
- Encourages food sharing.
- Promotes a sustainable community.

🚀 Future Enhancements
